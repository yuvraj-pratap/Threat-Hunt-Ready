# Facilitator Guide

## Purpose
Use this as a short awareness activity for employees, IT support, and introductory security staff. It builds safe decision-making; it does not certify independent incident-response capability.

## Suggested session (15–25 minutes)
1. Introduce approved reporting channels and explain that an indicator is not proof (2 minutes).
2. Complete the quiz (8–12 minutes).
3. Discuss evidence and escalation for selected scenarios (5–8 minutes).
4. Recap official reporting, account recovery, and response procedures (2 minutes).

## Discussion prompts
- What made the email suspicious?
- Why should unexpected MFA prompts be denied?
- Why is an unusual login a lead rather than proof?
- Which metadata could help validate an unusual transfer?
- How can responders preserve evidence and protect privacy?

## Adaptation and scoring
Replace generic references to approved channels with the organization's actual internal route before workplace use. Never include real credentials, private employee details, or confidential logs in a public repository. Treat scores as formative learning feedback, not the sole basis for employee evaluation.
